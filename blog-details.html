RAKHI GIFTS

A thread that feels like them.

Choose a rakhi around their personality, then make a shared memory part of the gift.

By Tyohaar Studio10 October 20262 min read
← Back to the journal

Notice their style

Start with the person, rather than the biggest gift. Think about the colours they wear, the details they notice and whether they prefer something simple or a little more expressive. A handmade thread or an initial rakhi can turn a familiar tradition into a personal gesture.

Celebrate each bond

If you are gifting to more than one sibling, give each rakhi its own thought. A coordinated pair can celebrate your bond while a different note for each person keeps the gift individual. Check the personalization spelling and preferred design with the maker before confirming.

Make the note personal

Add a note about a memory that belongs to both of you. A childhood joke, a favourite outing or a small thank you can say more than a long message. If you include sweets or chocolates, ask about ingredients, packaging and the intended delivery date so the gift arrives ready for your celebration.

A simple thread becomes personal when the details belong to your bond.
A FEW DETAILS TO REMEMBER

Before you make it personal.

  • Confirm initials and spelling before ordering.
  • Choose a note that belongs to your shared story.
  • Check the gifting date and destination.
