OUR STORY

A gift is a way
of saying “you matter.”

A thread tied around a wrist. A box opened at a family celebration. A message kept long after the treats are gone. These are the moments that inspire Tyohaar.

Our gifting studio brings handmade rakhis, festival hampers and personal touches together around one simple idea: a gift should feel like it was chosen for one person.
