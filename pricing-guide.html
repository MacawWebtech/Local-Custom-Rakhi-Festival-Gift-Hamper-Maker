BEFORE YOU CHOOSE

A few answers, upfront.

Are these the final prices?

These are starting prices. Confirm the exact contents, availability, personalization, applicable taxes and delivery costs with the maker before ordering.

Is delivery included?

Delivery depends on the destination, order size and requested date. Include your location in your quote request for a confirmed delivery charge.

Does personalization cost extra?

Some gifts include a personal detail in the starting price. Extra keepsakes, branding or special packaging may affect the total. Share your requirements for a confirmed quote.

Can I stay within a fixed budget?

Yes, start with your budget in the Hamper Studio and adjust the gift items and packaging. The estimate helps you plan; the maker confirms the final cost.

How is bulk gifting priced?

Quantity, contents, packaging, branding and delivery addresses shape bulk pricing. Share your order details on the Corporate Gifts page for a tailored enquiry.

How do I request a quote?

Complete the form below with your occasion, quantity and budget. Download the enquiry and share it directly with the maker to confirm your gift plan.
