TYOHAAR — Handmade Rakhi & Festival Gifts

Open index.html, or serve this folder with any static web server. The site uses HTML, the Bootstrap grid, CSS and JavaScript.

Pages: index.html, home-2.html, about.html, shop.html, product-details.html, festive-collections.html, custom-hamper.html, corporate-gifts.html, pricing-guide.html, blog.html, blog-details.html, services.html, service-details.html, contact.html, login.html and register.html.

Palette: black #000000, white #FFFFFF and deep raspberry #B10B51. Fonts: Cormorant Garamond (headings) and Manrope (text), both stored locally in assets/fonts.

Images: keep the existing filenames in assets/images to replace a photo. Page heading photos live in assets/images/background.

Testimonials (About page): replace assets/images/testimonials/testimonial-1.jpg, testimonial-2.jpg and testimonial-3.jpg with your own photos (portrait 4:5, around 800 x 1000 px). Edit the quotes and names directly in about.html, in the section with id="testimonials".

Studio location: Edison, New Jersey, USA. It appears in every footer (class "footer-location"), in the Studio Visits card and the "Find us" map on contact.html (id="studio").

Enquiries download as text files for the customer to share with the maker. Sign-in and registration are not connected to an account service yet.
