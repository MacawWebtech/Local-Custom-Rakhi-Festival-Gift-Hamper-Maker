BEFORE YOU GET IN TOUCH

A few things to know.

How do I share my enquiry?

Download your enquiry packet and send it through your usual contact with the maker. The files are prepared on your device.

What files can I include?

Add up to three JPG, PNG or PDF reference files, each up to 10 MB. Include only the details needed for your gifting brief.

Is a consultation immediately confirmed?

No. Share the request with the maker to agree availability. The calendar file marks the preferred time as tentative.

Can I request a custom or bulk order?

Choose the enquiry type and share your quantity, budget, preferred date, personalization and delivery requirements.
